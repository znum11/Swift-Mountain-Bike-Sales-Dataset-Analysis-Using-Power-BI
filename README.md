# Swift-Mountain-Bike-Sales-Dataset-Analysis-Using-Power-BI
This analysis will focus on identifying the most and least profitable products and categories, assessing sales performance across different regions and customer segments, and exploring the relationships between various factors like price, cost, and sales volume to inform strategic business decisions.

Objective: The primary objective of analysing this dataset is to gain a comprehensive understanding of bike product profitability and sales performance by evaluating factors such as product description, category, sales quantity, revenue, cost, and geographical distribution. 

Data Cleaning and Transformation 
•	Handled null values and ensured data consistency. 
•	Standardised formatting for better visualisation. 
•	Names Columns more accurately
•	Added new columns to distinguish colour. Added another column to assess the sales category of the bike.
Dashboard Design 
•	Designed an intuitive layout with multiple pages. 
•	Used slicers for interactive data exploration. 
•	Implemented KPIS and charts to highlight key metrics. 
Key Insights and Visualisations:
This dashboard for "Swift Mountain Bikes" presents a high-level overview of their performance, showcasing a total revenue of 362.91K, 89 total orders, and a total profit of 165 K. Key visualisations include a map displaying revenue generated by country, a line chart showing orders made by color (Black and Silver), and a pie chart illustrating customers by age group, with the largest segment being Adults (35-64). Additionally, bar charts analyse revenue by customer gender and sales category, and revenue by country and customer gender, providing insights into geographical performance and customer demographics.
